privacy

Cleaned up on your phone, not on a server.

ReaderFlow downloads the page and pulls out the article on your device. There's no reader service in the middle, so nobody else sees what you save.

offline

Already there when the signal isn't.

The text is stored with the article. Read on a plane or underground, and pick up at the same paragraph next time.

New on Mac

Your reading list,
from the terminal.

The Mac app includes a readerflow command. Save links from scripts, list what's waiting, and print any article as clean Markdown.

Download for Mac
~ — zsh
$ readerflow add https://example.com/slow-web --category essays --wait
Saved “Notes on a slower internet” · essays
 
$ readerflow list --unread --limit 3
-217361609 Notes on a slower internet essays
1388100523 How SQLite does WAL dev
-40077321 A field guide to sourdough kitchen
 
$ readerflow get -217361609 --format markdown
# Notes on a slower internet
Most of what we save, we never read…
 
$
add
Save a URL, optionally into a category. --wait returns once the text is ready.
list
Filter by unread, archived, category or a search term.
get
Print an article as JSON, HTML, Markdown or plain text.
tag · untag
Move an article in or out of a category.
archive
Mark it done. unarchive brings it back.
random
Can't decide? Get one unread article.

Works with AI agents. Install the ReaderFlow skill and your agent can save links, summarize what's waiting, and tidy up your categories. Every command has a --json mode.
